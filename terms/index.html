Terms of use

Last updated November 18, 2026.

What you get

A weekly email listing business fit-out permits approved in the city you chose, taken from that city's open data.

Price and cancelling

  • The first 2 weeks are free.
  • After that it is $99 a month for each city, billed monthly.
  • You can cancel at any time by replying to a list. You are not billed again after you cancel.

How you may use a list

  • Use it to offer your own goods or services to the businesses listed.
  • Follow Canada's anti-spam law when you email a business.
  • Do not resell or share the list.
  • Edmonton records are the exception: they stay under the City of Edmonton's Open Data Terms of Use, and nothing here restricts them further.

Accuracy

The records come from each city as they are. They can be late, incomplete or wrong, and a permit does not guarantee that the work goes ahead. Early Notice does not promise that any record leads to a sale.

The cities

Each record stays subject to the open data licence of the city it came from, listed on the Sources and licences page. Early Notice is not affiliated with or endorsed by any city.

Removals

A business can ask for its address to be removed from every list. See Remove a listing.
