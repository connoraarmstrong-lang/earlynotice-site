Terms of use

Last updated November 20, 2026.

What you get

A weekly email listing business fit-out permits approved in the city you chose, taken from that city's open data, and a private map of those records where the city publishes each permit's position.

Price and cancelling

  • The first 2 weeks are free. You give a card when you start. It is not charged until the free weeks end.
  • After that it is $49 a month for your first city, charged to that card each month until you cancel, or $490 for a year paid up front.
  • Each city after your first is half that price.
  • Your price does not go up while your subscription continues without a break.
  • You can cancel at any time, from the link at the bottom of any list we send you or by writing to us. You are not billed again after you cancel. Cancel during the free weeks and you are not charged at all. If you cancel a yearly plan, we refund the full months you have not used.
  • Payment is taken by Stripe on its own pages. We never see or hold your card number.

If the service changes or stops

  • The service depends on each city continuing to publish its permit records. If a city stops, changes or delays its records, the list for that city may shrink or stop.
  • We may change or end the service, or stop serving a city, at any time. If we do, we tell you by email.
  • If we end your subscription or stop serving your city, you are not billed again. On a yearly plan we refund the full months you have not yet received.

The limit of what we owe

That refund is the whole of what Early Notice owes you if the service stops, changes or falls short of what you expected. Early Notice is not responsible for lost sales, lost profit or other knock-on losses. In every case its total responsibility to you is limited to the amount you paid in the 12 months before the claim.

How you may use a list

  • Use it to offer your own goods or services to the businesses listed.
  • Follow Canada's anti-spam law when you email a business.
  • Do not resell or share the list.
  • Edmonton records are the exception: they stay under the City of Edmonton's Open Data Terms of Use, and nothing here restricts them further.

Accuracy

The records come from each city as they are. They can be late, incomplete or wrong, and a permit does not guarantee that the work goes ahead. Early Notice does not promise that any record leads to a sale.

The cities

Each record stays subject to the open data licence of the city it came from, listed on the Sources and licences page. Early Notice is not affiliated with or endorsed by any city.

Removals

A business can ask for its address to be removed from every list. See Remove a listing.
