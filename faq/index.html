Questions and answers

Where does the information come from?

From building permits that cities publish as open data. Each city on Early Notice licenses that data for commercial use, and every city is credited on the Sources page.

What is in each record?

The address of the space, the kind of business where the city says, the date the work was approved and, where the city publishes it, the estimated cost.

Does it tell me who the business is?

Usually not. Most cities publish where the work is and what kind it is, not the name of the business moving in. Early Notice gives you the where and the when. A visit or a quick search gives you the who.

Is every record a brand-new business?

No. A fit-out permit also covers a business that is moving, expanding or renovating. All of them are changing their space, which is when they buy.

How fresh is it?

Most cities update daily or weekly, and a few update monthly. Each city page shows the date of its newest record. Where a city has a map, your map is refreshed every weekday, and the list arrives each Monday.

Which cities are covered?

Every Canadian city we found that publishes current permit data under a licence allowing commercial use. The Cities page lists each one with last month's count.

What does it cost?

$49 a month for one city, after 2 free weeks. There is no card to enter and nothing to sign up for during the free weeks. That is a founding price: it does not go up for as long as you stay. Pay for a year and two months are free ($490). Each extra city is half price.

Why only 5 businesses per trade?

A list is worth less when every competitor has it. In each city we take at most 5 businesses of each trade. When a trade is full, we keep a waiting list.

How do I start, and how do I cancel?

Email us with your city and we send the next 2 Mondays free. To cancel, reply to any list and it stops.

Can I get more than one city?

Yes. Each city is its own subscription, so you only pay for the places you work.

My business is on a list and I would rather it was not.

Tell us the address and we remove it from every list for good. See the Remove a listing page.

Are you part of the city?

No. Early Notice is an independent business. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any city.
