Privacy

Privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

What the lists contain

Business fit-out permits: the address of a business space, the kind of business, the date and the estimated cost, as each city publishes them. The lists do not include home addresses or the names of people.

What we keep about subscribers

Your email address, your business name and the city you chose, so we can send your list and bill you. We do not sell or share them.

If we emailed you first

We only write to a business at an address it has published on its own website. Reply "no thanks" and we do not write again.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. We cannot see who opens a page or a map.

The map pages load two things from other services: the map software from cdnjs, and the map pictures from OpenStreetMap. As with any website, those services see the internet address of the device asking for them.

Private maps

A subscriber's map is locked with a key that exists only inside their own link. Without that link the map cannot be read. Anyone the link is forwarded to can open it, so treat it like a password.

Removing a listing

If your business appears on a list and you would rather it did not, see Remove a listing.
